Features

Your life is the game. These are the mechanics.

⚡
XP & Levelling System

Every completed task earns XP. Stack enough and you level up, unlocking new character titles, rewards, and quest tiers. Your progress is always visible.

🔥
Streak Mechanics

Daily streaks multiply your XP earnings. Miss a day and it resets — but shield potions can protect streaks you've worked hard to build.

🏆
Badge Collection

80+ unique achievement badges across health, focus, social, creativity, and learning. Share your rarest ones to flex in the community.

⚔️
Guild Challenges

Form parties with up to 5 friends. Take on weekly boss challenges together — everyone must contribute or the guild fails. Accountability, gamified.

🗺️
Quest Storylines

Long-form quest arcs unlock over months. Complete 30 days of focused work and unravel a hero's journey narrative that mirrors your real growth.

📊
Skill Tree Analytics

Every habit maps to a skill tree: Fitness, Focus, Creativity, Social, Health. Watch your character's abilities grow as your real-world skills develop.
