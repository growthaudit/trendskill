How AURA Works

Intelligent comfort, passively.

🧠
Adaptive Learning

AURA observes your routines for 7 days, then begins pre-adjusting your environment before you even realise you're uncomfortable. It gets smarter every week.

🌬️
Air Quality Intelligence

Real-time CO₂, VOC, humidity, and particulate monitoring. AURA automatically opens vents, triggers purifiers, and notifies you of air quality issues before they affect you.

🌙
Sleep Optimisation

Tracks your sleep cycles via non-contact radar. Gradually dims lights, lowers temperature, and plays personalised soundscapes timed to your natural circadian rhythm.

⚡
Energy Intelligence

AURA shifts heating and cooling to off-peak tariff windows without compromising comfort. Average household saves 23% on energy bills in the first year.

🔗
Universal Compatibility

Works with 2,400+ smart home devices. Apple HomeKit, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Philips Hue, Nest, and more — AURA acts as the intelligent hub for everything.

🔐
Private by Design

All AI processing happens on-device. Your home data never leaves your network. We don't have servers that know when you wake up or leave the house.
