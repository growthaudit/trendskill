Our philosophy

We believe
type is the
loudest voice
in the room.

MERIDIAN was founded on the belief that most design is too timid. Brands shrink their headlines, tame their palettes, and round every corner until nothing edges. We go the other way.

Our process starts with language — what a brand says and how it says it. Then we build a visual system around those words. Typography first, always. Colour in service of type. Layout as rhythm.

The result is work that commands attention before anyone reads a word.
