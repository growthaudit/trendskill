Why VERDANA

Shopping that doesn't cost the earth.

📊
Independent Impact Scoring

Every product on VERDANA carries a transparent 0–100 ethical score covering environmental footprint, fair labour, supply chain, and packaging — assessed by our independent panel.

🌍
Carbon-Offset Delivery

We partner with Pachama to offset 100% of shipping emissions on every order. You'll receive a certificate with every delivery showing the trees planted on your behalf.

✅
Verified Brands Only

Every brand on VERDANA passes a 40-point due diligence process. No greenwashing. If a brand's practices slip below our threshold, they're removed — publicly.

🔍
Supply Chain Transparency

Scan any product's QR code to trace its journey from raw material to your door. Where was it grown? Who made it? What was their wage? You can see it all.

🤝
Community-Led Reviews

Verified purchase reviews filtered for product quality and ethical experience. Our community flags greenwashing, and those reports trigger re-audits within 30 days.

♻️
Circular Returns

Returns that would normally end in landfill are donated, refurbished, or composted through our brand partner network. Zero returned items go to waste.

How our ethical scoring works

VERDANA's scores aren't self-reported — they're independently assessed by a panel of sustainability auditors, labour rights specialists, and supply chain experts. Scores update annually, or when we receive a community flag.
