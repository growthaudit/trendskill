SIGNAL ARCHITECTURE

◈
Modular Routing

Virtual patch cables connect any signal source to any destination. Build signal chains as complex as analog hardware — without the rack space.

⟳
Wavetable Engine

Import custom wavetables or draw your own. 4096-point resolution with real-time morphing between 480 factory waveforms at sub-sample precision.

◉
Spectral Filter

FFT-based filtering with 1024-band resolution. Draw custom filter curves, animate them with LFOs, or let the AI generate evolving filter movement.

⌬
Neural Presets

Describe your sound in plain language. ARCWAVE's synthesis AI translates text into patch configurations, ready to tweak and make your own.

⚡
Zero-Latency Core

Custom DSP engine running on dedicated CPU threads. Sub-2ms round-trip latency at 192kHz sample rate. No ASIO drivers required on Mac.

⬡
Hex Sequencer

Six-track polyrhythmic step sequencer with per-step probability, velocity randomisation, and euclidean rhythm generation. Program in seconds.
