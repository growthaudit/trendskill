Who we are

A home for hands-on creativity

PATCHWORK started in 2022 as a Discord server for three friends who wanted to talk about risograph printing. Now it's home to 12,000 makers across 40 countries — all united by a love of physical, tangible, imperfect art.

We're not about polished portfolios or follower counts. We're about the joy of making something real with your hands and sharing it with people who get it.
